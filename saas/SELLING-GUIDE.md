# RestroGenie Cloud — Selling Guide (Go-To-Market)

How to price, package, and sell your POS SaaS to shops in India. Monthly subscription
model, with the playbook a solo founder can actually execute.

---

## 1. Pricing (monthly subscription)

Tuned for Indian SMB shops. Anchor on the **Pro** plan; most shops land there.

| Plan | Price | Best for | Key limits |
|------|------:|----------|-----------|
| **Free Trial** | ₹0 / 14 days | Everyone, to start | 1 branch, 2 users, 50 items |
| **Starter** | **₹499/mo** | Tiny shop, single counter | 1 branch, 3 users, GST billing, reports, printing |
| **Pro** ⭐ | **₹999/mo** | Most shops (the default) | 1 branch, 8 users, inventory, online orders, KOT/KDS, roles |
| **Premium** | **₹1,999/mo** | Multi-outlet / chains | Unlimited branches & users, all shop types, API, priority support |

**Add-ons (extra MRR):**
- Extra branch: **₹299/mo** each
- WhatsApp bill/receipts: **₹199/mo**
- Aggregator (Swiggy/Zomato) auto-sync: **₹299/mo**
- Priority/onsite support: **₹499/mo**

**Discounts that protect MRR:** annual plan = **2 months free** (pay 10, get 12) — boosts
cash flow & cuts churn. Avoid ad-hoc % discounts; use the annual lever instead.

> Optional **one-time setup fee ₹2,000–₹5,000** (data entry, menu/product upload,
> printer setup, staff training). Many Indian shops happily pay for "done-for-you"
> onboarding — and it filters out tyre-kickers.

## 2. Unit economics (know these cold)

- **MRR** = Σ monthly price of active shops. **ARR** = MRR × 12.
- **ARPU** ≈ ₹900–1,100 (most on Pro + an add-on).
- **CAC** (cost to acquire): keep under ~2 months of ARPU (≈ ₹2,000) via field/referral sales.
- **Churn:** target < 4%/mo. POS is sticky once their daily billing runs on it — the
  switching cost (re-entering menu, retraining staff) is your moat.
- **LTV** ≈ ARPU ÷ churn. At ₹1,000 ARPU & 4% churn → LTV ≈ ₹25,000/shop.
- **Rule of thumb:** LTV : CAC ≥ 3:1. At the numbers above you're ~12:1 — very healthy.

**Milestones:** 50 shops ≈ ₹50k MRR (₹6L ARR). 200 shops ≈ ₹2L MRR (₹24L ARR).
500 shops ≈ ₹5L MRR (₹60L ARR) — a real business.

## 3. Who to sell to (ICP) & where to find them

**Ideal first customer:** a single-location restaurant/cafe or kirana/pharmacy that
currently bills on a calculator, a generic invoice app, or an old offline POS — owner
is on WhatsApp, wants GST bills, and feels the pain of stock/leakage.

**Channels, ranked by what works in India:**
1. **Field sales (highest converting):** walk into 10 shops/day in one market area. Demo
   on a tablet in 5 minutes. India POS is sold feet-on-street. Start in YOUR city.
2. **Referrals:** give a free month for every shop a customer refers. Shopkeepers in a
   market all know each other — one happy biryani place → the whole lane.
3. **Local associations / wholesalers:** restaurant associations, chemist associations,
   kirana federations. One talk = a room full of prospects.
4. **WhatsApp + Instagram/Facebook ads:** geo-targeted to shop owners; ad → 1-min demo
   video → "Reply DEMO". Cheap leads in tier-2/3 cities.
5. **Partners/resellers:** local accountants, billing-machine dealers, CA firms — pay
   them 15–20% recurring commission to resell. Force-multiplies a solo founder.
6. **Marketplaces / listings:** IndiaMART, JustDial, Google Business — inbound intent.

## 4. The sales motion (demo → trial → paid)

```
Cold visit / lead
   │  5-min tablet demo (their menu live in front of them — use the QR self-order wow)
   ▼
Start 14-day Free Trial  (you provision them in the super-admin panel in 60 sec)
   │  ← optional ₹2k setup: you upload their menu/products & train staff
   ▼
Day 3 check-in (WhatsApp) · Day 10 "how's it going?" · Day 13 "ready to continue?"
   ▼
Convert: Razorpay subscription (UPI-autopay) → Active
   ▼
Onboarding done → ask for a referral + a Google review
```

**Demo script (under 5 min):**
1. "Bill a table/customer in 3 taps" → print an 80mm bill with their name on it.
2. "Your stock auto-drops as you sell" → show low-stock alert.
3. "Owner sees today's profit on the phone" → dashboard.
4. "Customers order from a QR on the table" → the self-order page.
5. Silence. Let them ask the price. Then start the trial on the spot.

## 5. Onboarding & retention (where the money is kept)

- **Fast first value:** their real menu/products loaded within 24h. A shop that bills 50
  real orders in week 1 almost never churns.
- **Training:** one 30-min session + a 2-min WhatsApp video. Keep it stupid-simple.
- **Support:** a dedicated WhatsApp number. Response speed > everything for SMBs.
- **Monthly "your shop did X" email:** revenue, top item, profit — reminds them of value.
- **Win-backs:** payment failed → personal WhatsApp, not just a dunning email.
- **Feature requests = roadmap:** shopkeepers will tell you exactly what to build next.

## 6. Positioning vs competitors

You compete with Petpooja, POSist, Gofrugal, Vyapar, and "billing machine" dealers.
Your edges for the pitch:
- **AI-native** (forecasting, chat assistant, menu engineering) — they bolt it on, you bake it in.
- **Works offline** — bills keep printing when the internet dies; syncs later. Huge in India.
- **Multi-vertical** — one platform for restaurant *and* retail *and* pharmacy owners (and owners who run more than one).
- **No per-transaction fee** — flat monthly; they keep 100% of sales.
- **WhatsApp-first support** in their language.

Don't out-feature incumbents — out-**onboard** and out-**support** them.

---

## 7. Legal / trust basics (don't skip)

- **Terms of Service + Privacy Policy** (data ownership: the shop owns their data; you're
  the processor). Free generators exist; have a lawyer skim before scale.
- **Subscription terms:** billing cycle, cancellation, refund policy stated clearly.
- **Your invoices to shops** carry your GSTIN (register when turnover crosses the GST
  threshold; many B2B buyers want a GST invoice from day one).
- **Data deletion + export** on request (trust now; aligns with India's DPDP Act).
- **Uptime promise:** a simple SLA on Premium ("99.5%, credits if we miss") signals seriousness.

## 8. First 30 days — concrete plan

| Week | Goal |
|------|------|
| 1 | Deploy (HOSTING-GUIDE). Lock pricing. Make a 1-min demo video. List on Google Business. |
| 2 | Walk your local market: 10 shops/day. Aim for **5 trials**. Offer free setup. |
| 3 | Convert trials (WhatsApp follow-ups). Sign 1 reseller (a local accountant/dealer). |
| 4 | First **10 paying shops** (~₹10k MRR). Collect 3 testimonials + reviews. Iterate pricing. |

Then repeat the market-by-market motion. 10 → 50 → 200 is the same playbook, scaled.

**Sell the trial in person, win with onboarding + WhatsApp support, keep them with

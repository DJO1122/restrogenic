# 📣 RestroGenie — Promotion Guide

How to get shops using **restrogenic.shop**. India-focused, low budget, solo-founder friendly.

---

## 0. Before you promote (1 day)
- [ ] Live site works: `https://restrogenic.shop`
- [ ] Record a **60-sec demo video** on your phone: bill an order → print → show dashboard. This is your #1 sales tool.
- [ ] Make a **WhatsApp Business** number for support/sales.
- [ ] One-line pitch: *"Billing, GST & stock software for your shop — ₹499/month, 14 days free."*

---

## 1. Field sales (highest converting in India)
Shopkeepers buy when they SEE it work in front of them.
- Walk one market street. Enter 10 shops/day.
- 2-min demo on your phone/tablet: their menu, a live bill, the profit dashboard.
- Goal each visit: start a **free trial** on the spot (you create it in the super-admin in 60 sec).
- Carry: a printed one-pager (QR to restrogenic.shop) + your WhatsApp number.

**Best targets:** small restaurants, cafes, kirana stores, chemists still billing on calculator / old machine.

---

## 2. WhatsApp + referrals (cheapest growth)
- Send the **60-sec demo video** to local shop-owner groups.
- Referral offer: *"Refer a shop → both get 1 month free."* Shopkeepers all know each other.
- After a shop goes live, ask for a 5-star Google review + 2 referrals. Same day, while happy.

---

## 3. Social media (free reach)
- **Instagram / Facebook Reels:** post the demo video. Caption in Hindi + English. Hashtags: #restaurantbilling #POS #kirana #smallbusiness #[yourcity].
- **YouTube:** one 3-min "how to bill in RestroGenie" video. Ranks for "restaurant billing software".
- Post 3×/week: a feature, a customer photo, a tip. Consistency > polish.

---

## 4. Paid ads (when you have ₹3–5k to test)
- **Meta (FB/Insta) ads** geo-targeted to your city, interest = restaurant/retail owners.
- Ad → 60-sec video → button "Reply DEMO on WhatsApp".
- Start ₹200/day. Keep what brings trials, kill the rest.
- **Google Search ads** on "restaurant billing software", "GST billing app", "[city] POS".

---

## 5. Partners / resellers (force multiplier)
- Local **accountants / CAs**, **billing-machine dealers**, **POS hardware shops**.
- Give them **15–20% recurring commission** per shop they bring.
- One dealer can onboard 10+ shops you'd never reach.

---

## 6. Listings (free inbound leads)
- **Google Business Profile** — "RestroGenie POS, [city]".
- **JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha** — list as billing-software vendor.
- **Play Store note:** add the PWA / "Add to Home Screen" link.

---

## 7. Pricing to advertise
| Plan | Price | Pitch |
|------|------:|-------|
| Free Trial | ₹0 / 14 days | "Try free, no card" |
| Starter | ₹499/mo | single counter |
| **Pro** ⭐ | ₹999/mo | most shops (inventory + online orders) |
| Premium | ₹1,999/mo | multi-branch |

Lead with the **free trial** in every ad/post. Hook = free + works in 60 sec.

---

## 8. Selling points (use in every pitch — NO "AI" wording)
- ✅ Bill + GST invoice in seconds, thermal print
- ✅ Auto stock deduct + low-stock alert
- ✅ Swiggy / Zomato / phone online orders in one screen
- ✅ Daily sales + profit (P&L) reports
- ✅ Works **offline** — bills keep printing if net drops
- ✅ Flat monthly price, **no per-bill fee**
- ✅ WhatsApp support in your language

---

## 9. First 30-day plan
| Week | Target |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Demo video + one-pager. List on Google Business. Walk 1 market → 5 trials. |
| 2 | Follow up trials on WhatsApp. Sign 1 reseller. Post 3 reels. |
| 3 | Convert → first **10 paying shops**. Collect 3 reviews + referrals. |
| 4 | Start ₹200/day ads. Scale the channel that worked. |

**Goal day 30:** 10 paying shops ≈ ₹10k/mo recurring. Then repeat market by market.

---

### One-line strategy
**Sell the free trial in person, win with WhatsApp support + offline reliability, grow with referrals and resellers.**
